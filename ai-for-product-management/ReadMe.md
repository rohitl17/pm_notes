## Overview

This repository contains my structured notes from the **AI for Product Management** course by **Pendo × Google Cloud × Mind the Product**.

As a **Senior Data Scientist** working on GenAI, ML engineering, and large-scale AI products, this course helped me formalize how AI can strengthen:

- Product discovery  
- Roadmap prioritization  
- AI-powered features  
- Product-led growth (PLG)  
- Cross-functional decision-making  
- Customer-centric product strategy  

These notes are written from a **Data Scientist → Product-minded** perspective.

---

## Why These Notes Matter (Data Science → Product Management)

This course helped reframe my approach from:

> “How do I build a better model?”  
> to  
> “How do I build a better product that solves the right problem?”

### Data Science vs Product Management Focus

| Data Science Focus                        | Product Management Focus                                  |
|------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Model accuracy, latency, robustness      | User problems, business outcomes, product usability       |
| Datasets, features, pipelines            | User journeys, friction points, value creation           |
| Model evaluation (F1, ROC, MSE)          | Product metrics (activation, retention, conversion, NPS) |
| Deployment of ML services                | Delivery of end-to-end product experiences               |

These notes reflect how I integrate **AI/ML skills** with **product strategy**.

## Modules in This Repository

This repository is organized by modules from the course:

1. **Module 1 — AI for Product Managers (Fundamentals)**  
   - AI as a tool vs AI as a product capability  
   - Definitions: AI, ML, Deep Learning, NLP, Generative AI  
   - How PMs use AI across discovery, validation, analysis, and communication  

2. **Module 2 — The State of AI in Product Management**  
   - AI for data analysis, experimentation, and communication  
   - Where AI helps vs where human PM judgment is irreplaceable  
   - How AI frees up PMs to focus on strategy and customer empathy  

3. **Module 3 — Adapting Your Software Strategy in the AI Era**  
   - What should change, remain, and be newly created in your product with AI  
   - Internal and external context for AI strategy  
   - AI maturity levels: manual → assistive → AI-driven → fully automated  

4. **Module 4 — AI and the Product-Led Organization**  
   - What is a Product-Led Organization (PLO)  
   - How AI enhances product-led onboarding, self-service, and support  
   - Examples: OpenAI, Anthropic, Copilot-style products  

5. **Module 5 — Product-Led Growth (PLG) Principles**  
   - Free trials, freemium, and product-first experiences  
   - Delivering fast “Aha!” moments  
   - Usability, delight, natural upgrades, and virality  
   - How AI powers personalization, feature discovery, and sharing  

6. **Module 6 — Product Management Lifecycle (PMLC)**  
   - Phase 0: Define business outcomes  
   - Phase 1: Discover  
   - Phase 2: Validate  
   - Phase 3: Build  
   - Phase 4: Launch  
   - Phase 5: Evaluate  
   - Phase 6: Iterate  
   - How AI accelerates each phase of the lifecycle  

Each module has its own Markdown file with detailed notes and examples.

How This Course Improves My Data Science Work
1. User-Centric Perspective

Instead of only asking:

“Can we model this?”

I now also ask:

“Who is this for?”

“What problem are we solving?”

“How will we know it worked in the real world?”

2. Faster Discovery With AI

AI helps with:

Clustering support tickets and VOC data

Summarizing interviews and surveys

Identifying themes and pain points

Comparing retained vs churned users

This accelerates the Discover and Evaluate phases.

3. Better Validation and Prototyping

Use LLMs to quickly build interactive prototypes

Test concepts with real users earlier

Reduce the risk of building the wrong thing

4. Stronger Cross-Functional Communication

Using PRDs, user stories, and acceptance criteria, supported by AI tools, I can:

Communicate requirements more clearly

Translate technical details into business value

Align engineering, design, and GTM teams

5. Linking ML to Product-Led Growth

AI and analytics help:

Identify “Aha!” moments

Find behavior patterns linked to retention

Surface features that drive conversion

Support PLG strategies with data rather than guesses

Operational analytics dashboards tied to product metrics

For each, I now think in terms of:

Business outcomes

User journeys

Product lifecycle phases

PLG and product-led organization principles
