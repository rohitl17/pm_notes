# Module 1 — AI for Product Managers  
_Pendo × Google Cloud × Mind the Product_

## 1. How AI Supports Product Management

AI can impact product development in **two major ways**:

### **1. AI as a Tool for Product Managers**
Used to enhance PM workflows across:
- **Discovery** → user insights, clustering, text summarization  
- **Validation** → experiment simulation, demand prediction  
- **Analysis** → trend detection, forecasting, segmentation  
- **Communication** → automated updates, visualizations, storytelling  

These tools help PMs move faster, reduce manual work, and make more informed decisions.

### **2. AI as a Capability Built into the Product**
AI can also power product features, such as:
- Recommendation systems  
- Automated classification or anomaly detection  
- Predictive scoring models  
- Generative features (summaries, copilots, assistants)  
- Search and Q&A systems using LLMs  

This is where PMs decide: *What can AI unlock for the customer?*

## 3. Key Definitions

### **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**  
The ability of machines to perform cognitive functions associated with human intelligence.

### **Machine Learning (ML)**  
A subset of AI where algorithms learn patterns from data and improve over time.

### **Deep Learning (DL)**  
A class of ML that uses neural networks to process complex data like images, audio, and text with high accuracy.

### **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**  
Technology that enables machines to understand, interpret, and generate human language.  
Examples:
- Text classification  
- Chatbots  
- Document summarization  
- Q&A assistants  

### **Generative AI (GenAI)**  
AI systems that generate new content (text, code, images, audio).  
The most common form today is **Large Language Models (LLMs)**.

---

## 4. Large Language Models (LLMs)

LLMs are pre-trained on massive corpora of text and can:
- Generate text
- Answer questions
- Create charts or summaries
- Act as copilots for writing or analysis

They enable:
- Semantic search  
- Knowledge assistants  
- Code generation  
- Content automation  

---

## 5. How PMs Should Think About AI

PMs must evaluate AI as:

### **1. A Tool**
Used internally to make product work faster, better, and more scalable.  
Examples:
- AI-powered VOC analysis  
- Automatic synthesis of customer calls  
- Automated roadmap commentary  
- Market trend summarization  

### **2. A Capability**
Added into the product to deliver value to customers.  
Examples:
- An intelligent copilot for proposal writers  
- Predictive scoring for customer retention  
- GenAI assistants embedded into enterprise workflows  
- ML models powering risk, classification, forecasting  

The PM’s job is to determine:
- What problems AI can solve  
- Where AI adds real value  
- How to evaluate feasibility and risks  
- How to design AI-powered experiences responsibly  

---

## 6. Summary of Module 1

AI expands what PMs can build and how fast they can build it by:
- Enhancing analysis and discovery  
- Automating repetitive tasks  
- Delivering personalized, intelligent features  
- Opening new customer value through ML/GenAI capabilities  

PMs should continuously ask:
> *“Is AI making the product — or the product development process — meaningfully better?”*
