# 📘 Introduction to Product Strategy — Course Notes

This repository contains my structured notes from the *Introduction to Product Strategy* course by Great Learning.  
As a **Data Scientist**, understanding product strategy is essential for building solutions that not only work technically but also drive measurable business impact.

These notes summarize key concepts such as **Product-Market Fit**, **Value Proposition**, **Product Excellence**, and **Strategy Alignment**, all adapted through the lens of data-driven decision-making and AI product development.

---

## 🚀 Why Product Strategy Matters for a Data Scientist

Modern Data Scientists increasingly operate at the intersection of **product, business, and engineering**.  
A strong grasp of product strategy helps in:

### 1. Solving the *right* problems  
Data Science projects often fail not because of poor models, but because the problem wasn’t meaningful to the business.  
Understanding *underserved needs*, *value propositions*, and *market context* ensures models align with real customer problems.

### 2. Building solutions with measurable business impact  
Product strategy teaches how to tie every solution to business goals such as:
- Revenue growth  
- Cost reduction  
- User retention  
- Customer satisfaction  

This aligns perfectly with how DS/ML projects are evaluated.

### 3. Working backwards from outcomes  
Much like Amazon’s “Working Backwards” approach, data scientists benefit from:
- Clear definitions of **what**, **why**, and **who**  
- Prioritizing features and models based on value  
- Avoiding technical complexity that doesn’t deliver ROI

### 4. Designing user-centric ML systems  
Whether it’s a recommender system, risk model, or GenAI assistant:
- PMF thinking guides *what to build*  
- UX thinking guides *how customers use it*  
- Iteration/testing guides *how we refine it*

### 5. Communicating like a product partner  
As DS/ML moves closer to product engineering, product literacy helps you:
- Influence roadmaps  
- Communicate trade-offs  
- Translate insights into decisions  
- Collaborate with PMs, designers, and engineers
