# Crafting the Vision Statement

## 1. What Is a Product Vision Statement?

A **product vision statement** is a concise, inspiring declaration that answers:

> **“Why are we building this product?”**

It portrays:
- The purpose behind the product  
- The long-term aspiration  
- What the product aims to achieve for users and customers  
- How the product aligns with the organization's broader mission  

A great product vision becomes the **North Star** that guides strategy, prioritization, design, and execution.

---

## 2. Why Vision Statements Matter

A strong product vision helps teams by:

### 2.1 Prioritizing Work  
Teams constantly juggle:
- Feature development  
- VOC (Voice of Customer) work  
- Tech debt  
- Bug fixes  
- Roadmap planning  

The vision helps decide what matters *now* vs *later*.

### 2.2 Aligning Tactical and Strategic Decisions  
Every decision—from a sprint task to a roadmap epics—should tie back to the vision.

### 2.3 Maintaining Motivation  
A compelling vision keeps teams energized during long execution cycles.

### 2.4 Connecting to the Company Mission  
The product vision must ladder up to the organizational vision.

Examples:
- **OpenAI:** Make AI useful and deployed safely at a global scale.  
- **Fortive:** Build a smarter, safer, and more sustainable world.  
- **SolidCore:** Create a routine that transforms both fitness and overall life growth.  

---

## 3. How to Craft the Perfect Vision Statement

### Characteristics of a strong vision statement:
- **One sentence**  
- **Aspirational**  
- **Future-oriented**  
- **Independent of specific features**  
- **Emotionally resonant**  
- **Clear about the value created**  

### Vision Statement Template
> *“Enable [target user] to achieve [desired outcome] through [core idea or approach].”*

### Examples
- **SpaceX:** “Make life multi-planetary.”  
- **Google:** “Organize the world’s information and make it universally accessible and useful.”  

---

## 4. Measuring the Vision: OKRs

A vision lasts for many years. But to *measure* progress toward the vision, teams use:

### **OKRs (Objectives & Key Results)**  
A structured, quantifiable way to translate vision into measurable goals.

---

### 4.1 Objective  
- Inspirational, qualitative direction  
- Answers: **Where do we want to go?**

**Example (Facebook early stage):**  
“Connect people on the internet.”

---

### 4.2 Key Results  
- Quantitative indicators  
- Answers: **How will we know we’re succeeding?**

Examples:
- Daily Active Users  
- Number of interactions  
- Retention rate  
- First-week activation  
- Number of meaningful connections (Facebook later shifted here)

Key results **evolve over time** as the organization grows and the product matures.

---

## 5. Summary: Building a Strong Product Vision

To build a meaningful product vision, follow these steps:

1. **Define the opportunity space**  
   - Internal strengths  
   - External trends  
   - Problem–solution matching  

2. **Establish product principles**  
   - Specific  
   - Emotional  
   - Relevant  
   - Prioritized  
   - Concise  

3. **Build personas and empathy maps**  
   - Demographics  
   - Goals & challenges  
   - Buying motivations  

4. **Craft a clear, inspiring vision statement**  
   - One sentence  
   - North Star for all decisions  

5. **Develop strong communication habits**  
   - Reiterate vision frequently  
   - Align all messaging with it  

6. **Link the vision to success metrics**  
   - OKRs  
   - KPIs  
   - Engagement  
   - Adoption and retention metrics  

---

## 6. Summary: Course Wrap-Up

- **Product-Market Fit** is the degree to which a product fulfills a strong market demand.  
- Achieving PMF requires **vision, strategy, design, and execution**.  
- Without a strong **business strategy**, product decisions become suboptimal.  
- A robust product vision depends on:
  - Opportunity analysis  
  - Product principles  
  - Personas  
  - Vision statement  
  - Communication habits  
  - Success metrics  

Everything you build must ladder up to the **vision → strategy → execution → PMF** chain.
