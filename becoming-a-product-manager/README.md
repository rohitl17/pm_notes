# 🚀 Becoming a Product Manager: Course Notes & Data Scientist Perspective

This repository contains my organized and synthesized notes from the **LinkedIn Learning course, "Becoming a Product Manager."**

The primary focus of these notes is to bridge the gap between technical data science execution and strategic product leadership. They are specifically curated to enhance my capabilities as a **Senior Data Scientist** who manages end-to-end projects, engages directly with external **stakeholders**, and drives real **customer value**.

---

## 🌟 Why This Resource Exists

As a Senior Data Scientist, my role increasingly requires skills traditionally reserved for Product Management, including:

* **Translating business problems** into quantifiable data science initiatives.
* **Prioritizing features** based on ROI, technical complexity, and market needs.
* **Leading discovery** with stakeholders and defining clear acceptance criteria.
* **Communicating roadmaps** and managing expectations.

This resource serves as a personal PM playbook, ensuring that data science solutions are not just technically sound, but are also **viable products** that solve critical business problems.

---

## 📚 Core Topics Covered

The notes are structured around the product lifecycle, with specific emphasis on how each concept applies to **data-driven products** (e.g., ML models, analytics tools, recommendation systems).

* **Strategy & Vision:** Defining the **Why** and **What** of a product.
    * Market sizing, competitive analysis, and hypothesis generation.
* **Product Discovery & Customer Empathy:** Techniques for understanding user needs.
    * User interviews, prototyping, and Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD) framework.
* **Execution & Development:** Planning and managing the build phase.
    * Prioritization frameworks (e.g., RICE, MoSCoW), agile methodologies, and defining MVPs for data products.
* **Metrics & Optimization:** Measuring success and driving iteration.
    * Defining North Star metrics, A/B testing strategy, and leveraging data for continuous improvement.
